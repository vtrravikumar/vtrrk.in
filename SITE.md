# vtrrk.in — Personal Website

## Purpose

vtrrk.in is the personal digital home of V.T.R. Ravi Kumar.

It is not a resume site, corporate portfolio, or social-media replacement.

The site brings together Ravi's identity and body of work as an engineer, author,
photographer, traveller, rider, and builder.

## Primary Identity

V.T.R. Ravi Kumar

Engineer · Author · Photographer · Traveller

## Website Philosophy

- Person first, work second.
- Editorial rather than corporate.
- Personal rather than promotional.
- Visual rather than text-heavy.
- Simple rather than feature-heavy.
- Content should remain portable and owned.
- The website is the canonical home; social media is the distribution layer.

## Homepage

The homepage should provide:

1. Personal introduction
2. Current "Now" section
3. Books
4. Selected projects
5. Photography
6. Writing
7. Links to external platforms

## Visual Direction

The visual language should feel:

- Quiet
- Editorial
- Modern
- Personal
- Photographic
- Timeless

Avoid:

- Corporate portfolio aesthetics
- Generic developer portfolio layouts
- Excessive cards
- Animated gradients
- Stock imagery
- "Hello World" style introductions
- Skill-bar graphics
- Unnecessary animations

Photography should be treated as part of the site's visual identity.
