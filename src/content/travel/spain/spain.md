# Spain

This Spain entry was created from the Portugal journey as an initial record.

Pragati and I had originally planned to travel through Europe by road, with Spain as part of the journey. Circumstances changed the plan, and we travelled from Lisbon to Madrid by air instead.

After reaching Madrid, I explored the city and spent time photographing El Retiro Park, the Palacio de Cristal and the Madrid-Puerta de Atocha-Almudena Grandes railway station.

The original European road trip never happened, but Madrid still became an unexpected part of the journey.

*This story is intentionally kept as an initial version and will be expanded or refined later.*
