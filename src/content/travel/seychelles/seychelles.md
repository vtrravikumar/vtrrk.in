# Seychelles

A short, spontaneous escape from Dubai, made possible by the ease of travelling there without a visa. Pragati and I had only two days in Seychelles, but we spent almost all of them driving around Mahé and discovering what we could.

## Landing and getting on the road

We flew from Dubai on Emirates EK705, arriving in Seychelles early in the morning on 10 September 2016. At the airport we picked up a Suzuki Vitara rental from Absolute Cars and, with Google Maps as our guide, set off for the hotel.

The Crown Beach Hotel at Pointe au Sel was right beside the sea and had its own private beach. We reached the hotel and, rather than settling in, immediately set out to explore the island.

## Around Mahé

Most of our time was spent driving around Mahé. We visited Anse Royale Beach, Police Bay, Kempinski Beach and Bel Ombre, along with the Victoria Botanical Garden and the Sir Selwyn Selwyn-Clarke Market.

We also drove up to the Tea Tavern, a tea factory with its plantation nearby. The cool climate and occasional drizzle made the drives around the island particularly pleasant.

Mahé is small enough that you can cover a surprising amount of it by car in a couple of hours. For a two-day visit, we managed to see quite a lot.

## A familiar sight far from home

One of the things that surprised us most was the size of the Indian community in Seychelles. Many families of South Indian origin had come to the islands several generations earlier and had established businesses there.

Then we came across the Arul Migu Navashakti Vinayagar Temple. It was an unexpected sight in Seychelles — a very familiar South Indian temple, complete with the deity and the priest. Seeing something so recognisable so far from home was one of the memorable moments of the trip.

## Two days in Seychelles

Our two days were enough to get a good feel for Mahé, but in hindsight we probably spent too much of our limited time on the road. We were rarely at the hotel, despite having a beautiful setting right beside the sea and a private beach.

I sometimes think we should have stayed a little longer and slowed down — perhaps spent more time simply walking along the beach, enjoying the hotel, or trying something like snorkelling. Seychelles is the kind of place where two days can show you a lot, while still leaving you wishing you had stayed longer.
