# Kodaikanal

Sometimes a journey is simply about getting away from the familiar. For Pragati's birthday in August 2021, we decided to drive from our home in Chennai to Kodaikanal. Chennai's humid climate was reason enough to look for some relief in the hills, and Kodaikanal gave us exactly that.

We drove our Hyundai Creta from Chennai and spent the trip at The Carlton Kodaikanal. The hotel is very nicely located right next to Kodaikanal Lake, making it easy to step out for a walk or spend some time boating on the lake. We had booked a deluxe room for two with buffet breakfast and dinner and, apart from the accommodation, kept the trip deliberately simple.

We visited Kodaikanal Lake, a tea factory and the local market. There was no elaborate sightseeing itinerary. The hills, the cooler weather and the slower pace were really what we had come for.

For Pragati's birthday, we kept the celebration equally simple — just a cake cutting at the hotel.

The moment that stayed with me most was cycling around Kodaikanal Lake. The Carlton provided complimentary bicycles for its guests, and riding around the lake was one of those simple experiences that became the highlight of the trip. It was not something that required planning or a tourist checklist. It was just a beautiful way to experience Kodaikanal.

On the return journey we came through Trichy. The route gave me an unexpected opportunity to revisit my engineering college. The college was empty, but I took some time to walk around, visit my old computer lab and simply refresh memories of my college years. It was a very different kind of stop from the rest of the trip — a brief return to a much earlier part of my life before continuing home to Chennai.

Kodaikanal was therefore a quiet birthday getaway rather than a sightseeing-heavy holiday. A drive from Chennai, cool mountain weather, a comfortable stay beside the lake, a simple birthday celebration, cycling around the lake and, on the way home, an unexpected visit back to my engineering college. Sometimes that is quite enough for a memorable journey.
