# Switzerland

## The final stop of the journey

Switzerland was not a separate trip for us. It was the final stop of our 2016 European journey, which had started in Rome and taken us through Florence, Pisa, Venice and Milan before we reached Zurich.

After the movement through Italy, Zurich felt like a natural place to slow down before flying back to Dubai.

We stayed near the station, which made getting around easy. What struck me most about Zurich was its public transport. Buses, trams, trains and ferries seemed to fit together seamlessly, and everything was remarkably clean.

It was a very different experience from the cities we had just travelled through. We didn't have a long list of monuments to chase. Instead, we walked around, used public transport and simply enjoyed the city.

That was really what Zurich became for us — a comfortable final chapter to a journey that had begun with very little advance planning.

We had entered Europe through Rome and were leaving from Zurich. In between, we had discovered that we could travel between cities by train, book accommodation as we went and adapt our plans without needing to know exactly what the next day would look like.

Zurich reinforced that feeling. The transport was efficient, the city was clean and staying close to the station made it easy to explore.

After ten days on the road through Italy and Switzerland, we boarded our Emirates flight from Zurich back to Dubai on 25 August 2016.

Switzerland therefore wasn't really another destination added to the itinerary. It was the place where the Italy journey came to an end — and another small step in learning how we wanted to travel.
