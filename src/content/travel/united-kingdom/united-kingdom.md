# United Kingdom

## London, friends and a road trip through Scotland

Our United Kingdom trip in June 2017 was different from many of our earlier journeys. Pragati and I were travelling together, but this time we were also spending a good part of the trip with close friends. Prasannaa had recently moved into his home in Ilford and insisted that we stay with him and Kokilavani for the entire trip rather than book a hotel.

That hospitality became an important part of the journey.

We flew from Dubai to London Heathrow on Emirates flight EK003 on 1 June 2017. Prasannaa came to pick us up at the airport, and from Heathrow we went directly to his home using London's rail network.

The first couple of days were spent exploring London with Prasannaa. We visited Westfield Stratford City on the first day and Greenwich Park on 2 June. It was an easy introduction to the city, with the London Underground becoming our main way of getting around.

On the morning of 3 June, the trip changed character. We left London for a four-day Historic Scotland tour organised by Star Tours. Prasannaa and Kokilavani joined us, so what had started as a trip for two became a proper group journey.

The road trip itself was one of the highlights. We travelled north and reached Glasgow by evening, staying at Jurys Inn Glasgow as part of the tour. Over the following days we visited Glasgow Cathedral and George Square, travelled through the Scottish countryside and reached Edinburgh. Loch Tulla was another memorable stop, and we also visited Glengoyne Distillery.

The return journey brought us back to London on 7 June. There was still time to explore more of the city before our flight home. We visited the London Eye, Trafalgar Square, Big Ben, St Paul's Cathedral and Madame Tussauds.

Getting around London was straightforward because we mostly used the Underground. Food was never much of a problem either. We could always find an Indian restaurant, which made travelling with familiar food preferences particularly easy.

What I remember most about this trip, though, is not any single landmark. It was the combination of travel and friendship. Prasannaa and Kokilavani were wonderful hosts, and their insistence that we stay with them made the journey feel less like a conventional holiday and more like visiting friends while discovering a new part of the world.

It was also one of the relatively rare occasions when we travelled as a larger group rather than just Pragati and me. The Scotland road trip, the shared experiences and the time spent together in London made the trip memorable in a different way.

We finally flew back from London Heathrow to Dubai on Emirates flight EK032 on 10 June 2017.
