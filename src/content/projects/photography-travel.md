## What is it?

Photography & Travel brings together visual work and travel experiences: photographs, places explored, journeys taken and the stories behind selected moments.

## Why?

Photography is a way to observe more carefully and preserve the character of a place or experience. Travel provides the opportunity; the photographs and notes make those experiences shareable.

## Status

This portfolio area is established and the current body of work is considered complete for now. New journeys and photographs may be added over time.
