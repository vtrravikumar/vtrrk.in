## What is it?

**Vichar — विचार (Vicāra)** means *thought, reflection, consideration, deliberation, or idea*.

Vichar is an AI writing companion for X that helps turn an idea into a concise expression while keeping the user in control.

Its brand signature is:

**विचारय। आकारय। स्वकीयं कुरु।**  
*Think it. Shape it. Make it yours.*

The product is deliberately an assistant rather than an autonomous publisher. The user reviews and edits the generated text and makes the final decision to post.

## Why?

Good ideas do not always become good posts. Vichar is intended to remove some of the friction between having a thought and expressing it clearly.

The experience is built around topics that naturally fit my interests:

- Technology & AI
- Photography
- Royal Enfield & Riding
- Travel & Exploration
- Life & Observations
- Surprise me
- Custom topics

The generator can use optional context and different writing styles to produce concise starting points rather than generic social-media copy.

## Current status

The Vichar web experience is live on vtrrk.in.

It supports:

- topic-based generation;
- custom topics;
- optional location/context;
- varied writing styles;
- editable generated text;
- a 140-character limit;
- generating another variation;
- sending the edited text to the X composer for final review.

The Vichar Chrome extension is also implemented and has been submitted to the Chrome Web Store for review. It brings the same writing-assistance workflow into the X composer while keeping publishing manually controlled.

## Product principle

Vichar helps with the **thought and expression** — but the final words belong to the user.

It does not automatically publish to X.
