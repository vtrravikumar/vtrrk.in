## What is it?

Ride Together is a platform being built around the practical problems of organising and managing group motorcycle rides.

The project brings together the coordination that happens before a ride with the visibility and communication needed while the group is on the road.

## Why?

Group rides become harder to manage as the number of riders grows. Registration, participation, coordination and knowing where the group is can all become fragmented across different tools and conversations.

Ride Together is an attempt to bring those pieces into one purpose-built experience.

## Current status

The project is under active development. The current work is focused on establishing the application and API foundations before expanding the broader ride experience.
