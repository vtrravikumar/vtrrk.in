## What is it?

TweetPilot is a project exploring an approval-first workflow for creating and managing posts for X. It brings together daily suggestions, editing and approval, with the intention that only user-approved posts are published.

The project also provides the technical foundation for Vichar's writing-assistance experience.

## Why?

The goal is to reduce the friction of maintaining a thoughtful publishing routine without handing over the user's voice or publishing decisions to automation.

## Status

The core project foundation is complete for now. The user-facing Vichar experience continues as a separate active project.
