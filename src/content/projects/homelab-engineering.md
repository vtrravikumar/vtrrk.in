## What is it?

HomeLab Engineering is a personal engineering laboratory and a record of building an intelligent home.

The technology includes Raspberry Pi, Home Assistant, networking and automation. The project documents the engineering decisions, experiments, failures, recoveries and improvements that shaped the system.

## Why?

After a long software career, the HomeLab became a way to continue learning by building things rather than simply consuming technology.

The work demonstrates how curiosity can become disciplined engineering practice: observing carefully, experimenting, learning from failure, documenting decisions and simplifying systems so they remain dependable.

## Status

This project has reached a meaningful milestone for now. The HomeLab can continue to evolve, but the current body of work is considered complete.
