# vtrrk.in — Feature Catalogue

This document defines the intended feature set. It is not a statement that every feature is already implemented.

## F01 — Personal Home

The home page introduces Ravi and provides a curated view of current interests and work.

**Priority:** P0

## F02 — About

A narrative profile explaining the person behind the site, beyond résumé-style employment history.

**Priority:** P1

## F03 — Now

A current snapshot of what Ravi is focused on.

**Priority:** P0

## F04 — Books

A dedicated area for published books with individual book presentations and external purchase/read links.

**Priority:** P0

## F05 — Writing

An owned publishing area for essays, reflections and other long-form writing.

**Priority:** P0

## F06 — Projects

A curated presentation of projects Ravi is building, exploring or has built.

**Priority:** P0

## F07 — Photography

A dedicated visual destination for photography, initially able to connect to external collections and later support owned galleries.

**Priority:** P1

## F08 — Travelogue

A dedicated travelogue covering roughly 20 countries visited over approximately 20 years.

The feature should feel like a collection of personal stories rather than a generic travel guide.

**Priority:** P1

## F09 — Travel Place Pages

Each significant destination can have its own page containing narrative, visit context, photographs and observations.

**Priority:** P1

## F10 — Travel Discovery

Browse travel by country/place and potentially trip, year, theme or riding route as the collection grows.

**Priority:** P2

## F11 — Related Content

Connect books, writing, projects, photography, travel and rides when the relationship adds context.

**Priority:** P2

## F12 — External Presence

A curated list of relevant external platforms such as X, Instagram, 500px, Amazon and GitHub.

**Priority:** P1

## F13 — Contact

A simple way for people to reach Ravi.

**Priority:** P1

## F14 — Search Engine Foundations

Page titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph metadata, sitemap and other sensible SEO foundations.

**Priority:** P1

## F15 — Accessibility

Semantic markup, keyboard access, alt text, focus states and sensible contrast/motion behaviour.

**Priority:** P1

## F16 — Performance

Fast static pages, optimised images and minimal client-side JavaScript.

**Priority:** P1

## F17 — RSS / Feed

Optional feed for writing and/or other chronological content.

**Priority:** P3

## F18 — Map-based Travel View

Optional visual map showing countries/places visited.

This should enhance the travelogue rather than replace the narrative pages.

**Priority:** P3

## F19 — Search

Optional site-wide search once the volume of writing and travel content makes it useful.

**Priority:** P3

## F20 — Analytics

Privacy-conscious analytics may be considered after the core site is stable.

**Priority:** P3

## F21 — CMS

A CMS is explicitly not part of V1. Reconsider only if repository-based publishing becomes a genuine burden.

**Priority:** P4

## F22 — Newsletter

Not part of V1. Consider only if there is a clear publishing/distribution strategy that warrants it.

**Priority:** P4

## Feature Design Rule

Features should earn their place by improving the experience of understanding Ravi or exploring his work. The site should resist feature accumulation for its own sake.
