## Epilogue

A journey rarely ends when we return home.

The places may become photographs, the tickets may disappear into drawers, and the details may slowly fade. But some journeys remain with us in ways that are difficult to explain. A road, a conversation, a meal, a missed turn, a sudden snowfall, a quiet morning, or an unexpected moment can stay in memory for years.

Looking back across these travels, what I remember most is not a list of destinations. It is the accumulation of moments — the people who travelled with me, the colleagues who helped along the way, the plans that changed, and the small surprises that made each journey its own story.

Perhaps that is why I kept travelling, and why I kept photographing.

Travel gave me a way to look at the world, but returning home gave me a way to understand what those journeys meant.

These stories are my attempt to bring a little of that world home.
